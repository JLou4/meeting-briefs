## SkyPortal & LSST/Rubin Observatory

**SkyPortal** (skyportal.io) — open-source collaborative platform for time-domain astronomy that Bloom co-created. Published in *Journal of Open Source Software* and *Astrophysical Journal Supplement Series* (2023). Used by major astronomical survey teams for real-time transient classification, alert brokering, and multi-team coordination. 18 repos on GitHub, actively maintained.

**LSST/Vera C. Rubin Observatory:** Bloom was **co-chair of the Transients and Variable Stars science collaboration** — one of the most important working groups for the $700M+ telescope project that will scan the entire visible sky every few nights starting operations. SkyPortal was built partly to handle the LSST data firehose (~10M transient alerts per night).

**Why this matters for Valency:** Bloom built infrastructure to handle massive scientific data flows *before* — SkyPortal for astronomy, now Valency for all of science. Same pattern: data infrastructure layer enabling collaborative analysis at scale.

1. **Repeat founder with an exit.** Wise.io → GE proves he can take academic ML research to commercial acquisition. Not a first-time founder.
2. **The arXiv hallucination problem is a real market.** 35M researchers worldwide, LLMs hallucinating citations is a documented crisis (multiple journals now banning AI-generated references). Valency is building the grounding layer.
3. **Incumbents are weak.** Semantic Scholar (Allen Institute) is nonprofit. Google Scholar hasn't meaningfully innovated. Elsevier/Springer are legacy. The LLM-native research infrastructure layer is wide open.
4. **His domain expertise IS the moat.** Bloom isn't a tech founder bolting AI onto science — he's a top-cited scientist who's been building ML-for-science tools for 15+ years. SkyPortal → Valency is a natural evolution.
5. **National lab partnerships = distribution.** If Valency is already embedded with Lawrence Berkeley Lab users, that's a wedge into the entire DOE national laboratory system (~$20B/year R&D spend).
6. **Thesis fit:** AI infrastructure for science, applied ML, knowledge grounding — sits at the intersection of AI + deep-tech that Jack invests in.

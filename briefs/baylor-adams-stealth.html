The Z Fellows Context (his platform for the last 3 years)

To read Baylor, you have to understand Z Fellows — it is his network and his model:

  • Cory Levy's one-week founder fellowship for very early technical builders — small batches (~10 people).
  • Optional $10K check at a $1B cap; you can participate with or without funding, no equity required at the fellowship stage.
  • Low-commitment sprint, not YC: don't quit your job/school — daily standups, office hours, and talks with heavy-hitter founders/investors instead of a demo day.
  • Absurd top of funnel: high-schoolers, college students, soon-to-be dropouts, recent grads — "before the world caught on."
  • Alumni / network: founders tied to companies like Cursor, Cognition, Etched, Mach, Whop, Mintlify, Greptile, Cal AI, Nucleus; mentor pool name-drops Bechtolsheim, Thiel, Botha, Randolph, Hartz, Naval, Dylan Field, Guillermo Rauch, Stanley Tang.
  • Aggregate outcome: Z Fellows companies have raised $1B+ from top funds.
The takeaway: Baylor's edge isn't capital — it's proprietary access to the earliest-stage technical-founder funnel in the country, plus a warm relationship graph into the mentors/investors above. Whatever he's building now, that funnel is the asset.

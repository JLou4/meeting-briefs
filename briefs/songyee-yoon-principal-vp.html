TL;DR

Songyee is an AI PhD turned gaming executive turned VC. She earned an MIT PhD in computational neuroscience at 24,³ was President and Chief Strategy Officer of NCSoft,² and in December 2024 launched PVP, a $100M fund writing checks from $100K to "single-digit millions" into AI-native startups.¹ Her partners and advisors are unusually academic: Daniela Rus (head of MIT's AI and computer science lab, CSAIL), Dawn Song (UC Berkeley security researcher and MacArthur Fellow), and Jeremy Nixon (ex-Google Brain, founder of AGI House).¹⁴ She joined HP's board in February 2025.²

Why it matters for TBC: PVP's recent deals overlap heavily with your thesis: nuclear (Aalo), defense (Perseus), robotics and physical AI (Veeda, HYPRLABS, Venti), and AI compute (Infinity, Hyperbolic, Lambda).⁵ That makes her a natural co-investor and source of deals.

Thesis

  • AI-native, not AI bolted onto existing products. Her argument: more than half of today's top-10 NASDAQ companies were founded after broadband arrived, so the next giants will be built around AI from day one.¹
  • Targets foundation models, developer tools, middleware, applications, consumer tech and games.⁵ She has also named insurance, logistics and healthcare as industries AI will remake.⁶
  • Her three filters: durability, defensibility and real-world impact. She favours multipurpose infrastructure and companies that can build a data flywheel, meaning the product gets better as it collects more data.⁷
  • Expects the AI build-out to overshoot, as railroads and fiber did, but says the real value is in applying AI to actual business problems.⁷

Background

  • Education: BS in electrical engineering from KAIST, PhD in computational neuroscience from MIT (finished at 24), and a JD from Santa Clara University.²³ She was the real-life inspiration for the "genius girl" character in the Korean TV drama KAIST.¹
  • Early career: McKinsey. In 2004, at 29, she became SK Telecom's youngest executive and its first woman on the executive team.³
  • NCSoft: Chief Strategy Officer, then CEO of NCSoft West, then President from 2015. She led NCSoft's global expansion and its adoption of AI,² and founded Chamaeleon, NCSoft's corporate venture arm.⁷ She is married to NCSoft founder Kim Taek-jin.³
  • Boards and affiliations: HP Inc. (since February 2025), the MIT Corporation, South Korea's Presidential Advisory Council for Science and Technology, and the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace (trustee).²³
  • Writing: Push Play: Gaming for a Better World (Forbes Books, 2024)⁹, a Forbes column on gaming and AI, and an essay warning that generative AI risks "cultural colonialism".¹⁵

Recent views

"The power of compounding is not just in finance, but also in human capital, our understanding of technology, and also in relationships."⁷
  • On the AI bubble: she thinks overbuilding happens with every new platform (railroads, fiber), and that the money is in applying AI to real business problems.⁷
  • On AI spending: on Bloomberg in July 2026, she questioned whether hyperscalers will cut back as AI infrastructure keeps getting more expensive, and noted that chip makers and hyperscalers are on diverging spending cycles.⁸
  • On whose data trains AI: about 35% of the world has no broadband, so their cultures go unrepresented in training data.¹
  • On female founders: "We don't say it's a female fund, but… a lot of the female founders come to us."¹

Conversation angles

  • Energy for AI: ask what convinced her on Aalo, and connect it to your view that PJM's power market discourages long-term investment and that the real bottleneck is power, not chips.
  • Physical AI: Veeda, HYPRLABS and Venti all target the robotics data and autonomy problem. Ask whether she thinks simulation (world models) or real-world data wins. This links to the Object Zero idea that robots, not chips, are the second wave of power demand.
  • Defense: ask how an AI-native fund ended up backing counter-drone missiles (Perseus), and whether more defense deals are coming.
  • Her Bloomberg capex call: if hyperscalers do pull back, what happens to compute plays like Hyperbolic and Lambda?
  • Korea and Asia deals: ask about her networks through NCSoft, SK and Kakao, and whether PVP brings in Asian strategic co-investors.
  • Co-investing: PVP writes checks from $100K to single-digit millions, which leaves room for TBC alongside. Ask whether she ever brings in co-investors.

Who She Is — The 30-Second Version

Andrea Wong is one of the most accomplished Chinese-American media executives of her generation — an MIT-trained electrical engineer who became a Hollywood power player, then pivoted to professional board governance across entertainment, tech, and sports. She developed The Bachelor, Dancing with the Stars, and Extreme Makeover: Home Edition at ABC, ran Lifetime Networks as CEO, and oversaw Sony Pictures Television's international empire from London — including brokering the deal that brought The Crown to Sony.

Now ~58, she sits on the boards of Liberty Media (F1 parent company), Roblox, and Hudson Pacific Properties, with advisory roles at Workday, McLaren Racing, and Whalar. She calls herself a "pluralist" — deliberately cross-pollinating insights across gaming, motorsport, entertainment, and tech.

The archetype: engineer's brain in a storyteller's world. She left investment banking because it bored her, got hooked on TV after one morning in an NBC control room during her Stanford MBA, and spent the next 25 years proving that analytical rigor and creative instinct aren't opposites. She's now the rare person who can talk Roblox creator economics at breakfast and F1 aerodynamics at dinner — and connect the dots between them.
